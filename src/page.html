No double booking for AI phone agents

A free n8n booking tool by No BS AI for Vapi or Retell. It checks the calendar, books once, and refuses to double book, even when two callers grab the same time at once or the platform retries.

The question

On the n8n forum, people building AI receptionists keep hitting the same three walls: Vapi says "No result returned", the agent books a time that was already taken, and two callers both get the last slot.

Example: @imel, Nov 29, 2025: "The check_availability custom tool keeps returning "No result returned" error in VAPI." Thread. @RodrigoSyncData, Nov 13, 2026: "two callers asking for the last slot at the same moment can both get it." Thread.

How the workflow handles it

  1. Always answers HTTP 200 in the shape Vapi reads: results, toolCallId, one flat string.
  2. Re-reads the calendar right before it writes. Taken means nothing is booked and other times are offered.
  3. Reads the slot back after it writes. If two landed at once, the earliest wins and the other deletes itself.
  4. A retried call gets the same confirmation code, not a second booking.
